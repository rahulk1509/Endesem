Q1: Define Artificial Intelligence.
A: Artificial intelligence is the field of creating machines that simulate human reasoning, learning, and decision making.

Q2: What is supervised learning?
A: Supervised learning uses labeled training data so the model can map inputs to correct outputs.

Q3: Explain overfitting and underfitting.
A: Overfitting is excessive memorization of training data, while underfitting is failure to capture the underlying relationship.
