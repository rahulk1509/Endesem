Q1: What is Artificial Intelligence?
A: Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that can learn, reason, and solve problems.

Q2: Explain supervised learning.
A: Supervised learning trains a model on labeled examples so it can make predictions on new data.

Q3: Differentiate overfitting and underfitting.
A: Overfitting means a model learns the training data too closely, while underfitting means it is too simple to learn the pattern.
